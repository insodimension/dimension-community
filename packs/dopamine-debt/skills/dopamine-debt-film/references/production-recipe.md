# Production recipe: how an approved roast film was made

The reference run for a comedy explainer roast, from a one-line brief to a sealed render the reviewer approved ("there were no mistakes"). This file names every stage, the tool used, what it wrote in the production folder, and the trap met. SKILL.md is the order of work; this is the worked example.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Tools | Output (in the production folder) | Lessons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Research the facts** | `task` subagents (a lead plus parallel researchers, one per thread of the story), `web_search`, `read` on URLs, an archive for blocked pages | `research/facts.md` + `facts.json` (every entry with an id, ranked), `research/coverage.json` (the viewer questions) | Every URL opened, mirrors named. Ground rule: roast documented statements only; the premise is a labelled comic inference. |
| 2 | **Persona (the audience)** | YouTube comment pulls (top 400 by rank from reaction videos on the topic), Reddit threads, coding by subagents | the topic persona in the brand's `productions/dopamine-debt/personas/topic/` (card with source list and hash), its evidence beside it | The persona decides meme fit. Most rejected memes voiced the subject, not this audience. |
| 3 | **Spine and script** | hand-written, then repair passes | `script/spine.yaml` (packaging, premise, take, audience order, narrator, `anchor_world` with `lines`), `script/script.md`, `script/lines.json` (each line: text, speaker, pictures, facts, coverage) | Write for the core audience first, the adjacent ones second. `lines.json` is the one source the builder reads. |
| 4 | **Look** | HTML style boards | three candidate looks (`.html` + `.png`), cutout tests of the subject | Chosen: a fake desktop for the cold open, then the dark motion-graphic stage. |
| 5 | **Media sourcing** | browser capture (headless tab, highlight the quoted words), PyMuPDF for PDFs, `yt-dlp` (found by `framework/tools`) + `ffmpeg` for clips, Wikimedia Commons for photos | `media/captures/` (each page with `<id>.asset.json`: URL and capture date), `media/sourcing/` (creator clips and posts), `media/clips/*.json` (in/out, creator, URL, verbatim, `cuts`), `media/photos/` (+ `credits.json`, licences), `media/logos/`, `media/stock/` | Every source ≥ 720p. Commentary licence: short clip, credit in the description. One pick per creator. |
| 6 | **Memes** | the shared media library `<engine home>/media-library/<id>/entry.json` (read-only, corrections through `library_tag`) | keyed cutouts under `media/keyed/` | Never edit library entries directly (the strict schema rejects extra keys). Letterboxed or watermarked uploads get a `picture` rect on the film's MEME row. |
| 7 | **Voice** | `generation_submit`: model `elevenlabs/voice`, the brand's voice (`kit.voice.brand_voice`: the brand kit's `brand/voice.json`, else the Settings default), one `{voiceId, modelId}` pair on every job and never another model to fit time (G11 `brand-voice`), `options.production`; the pronounce map applied through `Film.spoken_forms`; each job recorded with `kit.voice.record_job` | `voice/<line>.mp3` + `.timing.json`, `voice/segments.json`, `voice/jobs.json` | Word timings lag the audio by up to 0.3 s: cut in the measured pause. A re-voice replaces one line without touching the rest. |
| 8 | **Music** | `generation_submit` model `elevenlabs/music` | `media/music/M1.mp3`, `M2.mp3` (+ asset records), registered in the brand's music ledger | Say which licence the bed is under. |
| 9 | **Build the cut** | the shared film kit `DD/framework/kit/` (README: the `Film` config, the `lines.json` contract, the API): `timeline.py` (lines → timed items, cuts in silence, `from`/`until` picture cues, clip jump cuts), `scenes.py` and `stage.py` (motion-graphic scenes cued to words), `compose.py` (HyperFrames HTML + `layout.json`), `DD/framework/overlays.py` (meme modes). The film's own part is `build/film.py` (its `Film`: poses, backdrops, desk, music cues, levels, pronounce map), `build/scenes.py` and `build/film.css` | `build/timeline.json`, `build/film/index.html`, `build/layout.json` | Scenes show something within 1 s; a counted tile shows "?" until its number; stamps go below their cards, outside the 10% edge band. |
| 10 | **Check before rendering** | `build/g11.py` → `DD/framework/kit/g11plan.py` → `DD/framework/g11.py` (`code_checks`; advisory `judge_checks` on the slow model via `completion`), `npx -y hyperframes@0.8.217 snapshot . --at t1,t2,…` (≤ 20 times per call on Windows) | `build/g11.json`, snapshots under `build/film/.hyperframes/artifacts/snapshots/` | Look at a snapshot of every changed picture. The check passed stages a viewer reads as empty; the blind log caught them. |
| 11 | **Render** | `build/render.py` → `DD/framework/kit/render.py` (HyperFrames picture, `kit/mix.py`, decode and A/V checks, `kit/instrument.py`) | `renders/<film>-1080p.mp4` (about 17 min to render), mix at −14 LUFS / under −1 dBTP, `proof/film-X/` (0.5 s contact sheets, Whisper transcript and words, `audio.txt` levels) | One heavy job at a time. |
| 12 | **Proof** | `task` blind reader (only the frames in `film-X`, never the script), coverage judge (`completion`: 5 default + 1 slow vote per question, strict majority), narration-ending check against the render's Whisper words, G11 with `film-X` (adds `no-dead-air`), faster-whisper on suspect windows | `proof/film-X/log.json`, `proof/coverage-results.json`, `build/g11.json`, `proof/PROOF.md` | A blind-log problem line is a defect until disproved (it once misheard a clip as silent; Whisper settled it). |
| 13 | **Deliver** | `build/description.py` (facts, pages, clip, meme and logo credits from the records), `present`, `control_resolve` | `youtube-description.md` | Show the person the render, the gate table with advisory findings, and what was not checked. |
| 14 | **Notes loop** | SKILL Notes mode: each note becomes a G11 rule with its check, or a labelled row in the brand's marks ledger; the check is run over the whole film | 10 marks → 5 new rules, 52 more instances fixed | Fix the class, not the mark. |

## Infrastructure used

- **Plugins and services:** the Video space media library (memes); the `media` generation provider (ElevenLabs voice and music); HyperFrames CLI 0.8.217 (composition, snapshot and render engine).
- **Session tools:** `task` (researchers, persona coders, blind loggers), `bash`, `read`/`edit`/`write`, `eval` with `completion` (judges), `present`.
- **Local binaries:** ffmpeg/ffprobe, yt-dlp, faster-whisper (CPU), PyMuPDF, numpy.
- **Shared code:** `DD/framework/kit/` (the film builder and mix), `DD/framework/g11.py`, `overlays.py`, `quiet.py`, `layout.py`; `DD/taste.json`; `DD/dd/gate.py`.

## Open gaps

- `meme-canonical-version` reads the library entry's `canonical.is`: fill it with `library_tag canonical {is, basis}` from the Know Your Meme page or the most-viewed upload; the person's switch on the Library page overrides it.
- `no-hr-music` and `no-text-walls` wait on Plan fields being added to `framework/g11.py`.
