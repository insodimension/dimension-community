# Stage recipes: files, schemas, commands

`DD` = this plugin's root; `WORK` = the brand's `productions/dopamine-debt/` (`dd.home.WORK` outside a gate run). A revision lives in `WORK/films/<f>/<rev>/`; the film's own production folder holds its kit build (`build/`), media, voice and renders.

## Layout of a revision
```
<rev>/
  WORKLOG.md                 checklist ticks with Exit outcomes (this skill)
  spine.yaml  intent.json    stage 1
  script/lines.json          joke map and script, one row per line (schema below)
  script/script.md           readable script, generated from lines.json
  script/build.py            lines.json + spine.yaml → framework/<stage>/{root,segments,intent,spine}.json (helpers in DD/framework/scriptstage.py)
  framework/script/          engine directory for the script read
  framework/seen/            engine directory for the SEEN read of the render (kit `seen.write`)
                             the build, voice and renders live in the film's production folder (kit-film)
```

## Engine directory (`framework/<stage>/`)
- `root.json`: `{"audiences": {"dry-run": "personas/channel/dry-run.md", "<topic>": "personas/topic/<topic>.md"}, "juror_model": "default", "stage": "<film> <stage>", "kind": "script|storyboard|cut|seen", "previous": "<path to the previous stage dir, relative to this one>" | null, "gated": [...]}`. `scriptstage.write_engine_dir(out, segs, intent, spine, audiences, label, kind, previous)` writes it. `kind: "seen"` turns on G12 and the whole-cut juror read; `previous` feeds G13; an ablation or what-if copy is `kind: "cut"`, `previous: null`, so it is never gated as a stage.
- `segments.json`, `intent.json`, `spine.json`: written by the revision's `script/build.py`.
- After a read: `cache.jsonl`, `jury-cache.jsonl` (bit jurors, plus `kind: watch` whole-cut reads at kind `seen`), `readouts.jsonl`, `jury.jsonl`. `dd.gate.check` writes nothing; end every stage with `dd.gate.write(film_dir, res)`, which writes `gates.json` (status, blockers, gates, score and `memes`: one row per meme or clip with gag, library, mode, display, replaces, t, bit, laugh + ½ smile) and `ledger.<aud>.json`. The next stage's G13 reads exactly those two files. `page.html` and `report.md` come from the revision's own driver, and `repairs.md` is written by hand.

## Commands
The house gate (no kernel): `bun <DD>/gate/launch.ts <script|cut> <production> <report.json>`; exit 0/1/2 as SKILL.md says. Inside a driver of your own, in Python with `DD` on `sys.path`:
```python
import dd.read, dd.gate
res = await dd.read.read(film_dir, judge_batch, completion)
g = dd.gate.check(film_dir, film, g11)
```
- `film` comes from `dd.read.load_film(film_dir)`. At the script and storyboard stages, pass `g11={}` and report G11 as not yet checked.
- `dd.gate.blockers(g)` prints the refusal list.
- Script stage timing: `dd.units.estimate_timing(segments, wps)`. `tunables.wps` is 3.3, but Inso's measured voice pace is 2.70 words/s. Time the engine directory at **2.7**, not 3.3: G2 (5 s, 10 s) and G7 (20 s gaps) are timing gates, and at 3.3 every bit reads about 18% shorter than the voice will make it. v4 is timed this way (`script/build.py` `VOICE_WPS`).
- Shared stage helpers: `DD/framework/scriptstage.py` (`on_screen`, `unspoken`, `picture_text`, `MODE_TEXT`, `write_engine_dir`), imported by each revision's `script/build.py` and by the house gate.
- The carries read (`dd/run.py`) sends the line plus the segment's `picture`. At the script stage there is no picture, so a retell fact counts only if the narration says it; from the storyboard stage on, a picture that prints the fact (the money object) carries it too. This is the lever for a G9 restatement the juror rejects as a list.
- The fitted juror map (`tunables.juror_cringe_map`) jumps from 0.176 at a reading of 0.35 to 0.818 at 0.40. A bit the juror reads at exactly 0.40 is both a veto (G3 for a written bit, advisory G3m for a meme bit) and a cost of 4 × 0.818 = 3.27 debt; at the end of the film that one bit also fails G10 (v4 pass 1: D at the end 2.16). Expect blockers to flip on 0.05 of juror noise there.
- The whole-cut watch juror (G12 keep watching) is noisy per prompt, not only per draw: the transcript carries every unit's start time, so any timing change re-draws all reads. On security-week r1 pass 18, moving one meme by 1.4 s moved a 24-draw mean from 0.438 to 0.401, and a set that passed on its first 6 draws (0.475) was 0.455 over 24. Before claiming a keep-watching pass near the line, draw at least 24 (`dd.run.WATCH_DRAWS`, extra keys per draw) on the final timing and report both means.
- Calibration pair (2026-11-05): `DD/calibration/targets/altman-ram-roast/seen` (approved; `previous: ../text`) and `DD/calibration/failures/security-week-r1/seen` (rejected; pass `json.load(g11.json)["checked"]` as `g11`). `python -m pytest tests` in `DD` re-checks both verdicts and laugh conservation in about 2 s; the reads are cached, so it makes no judge calls.
- Curve page: `dd.page.render(path, title, film, ledgers, per, fg, sc, frames=..., notes=...)`.

## lines.json row
`{id, act, speaker: narrator|clip, kind: voice|clip, text, bit, role: setup|punch|tag, mechanism, target, carries[R ids], sources[S/C/N/P ids], coverage[C ids], pictures[{k: DOC|MONEY|BROLL|POST|NEWS|GFX, what}], plants|calls|opens|pays, meme?}`
`meme = {library, franchise, mode: fullscreen|cutout|spotlight|slot|ghost, anchor, why, in, out, dur, edge_db[in, out], line, whisper_heard, line_source, replaces?}`

That is the script-stage row. A film built with the shared builder, `DD/framework/kit/`, reads a production `script/lines.json` whose picture kinds (`MG`, `DOC`, `BROLL`, `POST`, `CUT`, `TAG`, `STK`, `SFX`, `MEME`, `CLIP`), `from`/`until` word cues and MEME fields are specified in `DD/framework/kit/README.md` (`lines.json` contract), with the voice, clip, capture and music records it also reads.

Segment fields for memes: `library` and `gag_id` (both the media-library entry id), `mode` (what the builder drew), `replaces` (the gag id of the previous stage's meme this one takes the place of; G13), and at the SEEN read `display: full|cutout|ghost|inset`, written from the frames, never copied from `mode` (r1's Williamson and Gattuso were built as `cutout` and rendered as small boxed windows).

- Face box (video-editor 0.1.29+, dimension #2340): a picture entry may carry `face: {x, y, w, h}`, given as fractions 0–1 of the frame with the origin at the top left, measured on the payoff frame. Use it to centre a spotlight disc, crop a slot or anchor a ghost on the face. Sounds and faceless B-roll have no face box. Code older than 0.1.29 reads entries that have one as damaged.
- From video-editor 0.1.30 (#2343), `library_tag` can correct an entry's `payoffLine` and its `inSeconds`/`outSeconds`; the payoff second stays fixed. When F-10 measures a clip's whole spoken line, write that line and its silence-snapped in/out back with `library_tag`, so the library holds the full line and the next film starts from it.
## Sources
- Verified facts: `<film>/<rev>/build/sources_verified.json`, holding `sources` (S, filing grep with file and line), `calcs` (C), `news` (N, said as "reports say"), `posts` (P, with uid and full_text). Reuse a sibling revision's verified file only when the filing hasn't changed.
- A capture needs its source URL **and its capture date** for G11 `real-sources`. The post date alone doesn't count. The Anthropic posts (P01–P12) carry no capture date from v3; v4 stamps `captured` with the fetch time of the evidence file that holds the comment (`personas/evidence/reddit/<thread>.jsonl` or `comments/<video>.jsonl`, all 2026-11-03) and records the file as `capture_record`.
- Check the direction of every comparison you joke about against its numbers. v4's first draft said the CEO's April sale at about $26 had "great timing, for them", but investors paid about $59 a month later, so he sold too early; the topic juror caught it ("reads backwards").
- Captures (`capture.mjs`): anthropic.com re-renders on hydration and removes the marks (`firstMarkRect` null, "reading 'bottom'"); sec.gov EDGAR filings set `content-visibility:auto` on every page (blank screenshot, CDP timeout on a 14 MB S-1). Capture the page's own HTML rendered locally through `overrides.json` `urls: [file:///…]`: for a Next.js page, the fetched HTML with every `<script>` stripped and `<base href>` set; for an EDGAR filing, only the `height:792pt` page div that holds the quote, with `content-visibility:auto` removed. `sourceUrl` stays the live page. (METR film, 2026-11-08.)
- Receipt size is width-bound: the card scales to the page's line length, so a desktop capture of a long-line page gives small text. Capture at a narrow viewport (`capture.mjs` `zoom` 2.3 → ~700 CSS px, `noZoomOut`) and hide side panels with a `css` visibility override; the page's own mobile layout gives short lines and big text. EDGAR filings cannot reflow (absolutely positioned lines): their receipts stay small, say so. Give each DOC a `read` band of the highlight plus one whole line each side until `read_geometry`'s context tolerance is fixed (gotchas 2026-11-08). (METR film, 2026-11-08.)

## Memes
- Library: `~/.inso/media-library/<id>/entry.json` (`file`, `payoff{line, t}`, `inSeconds`, `outSeconds`, `durationSeconds`, credit and franchise). `payoff.line` is often one word, so never treat it as the meme's line. `dd.read.load_film` resolves each segment's `library` against it (`film["library"]`); an id that starts `fetch-` or has no `entry.json` is not a library meme for G3m, G12 or a G13 replacement.
- `DD/framework/quiet.py`:
  - `speech(src, a, b)` returns `[word, start, end]` triples (faster-whisper, cached in `framework/speech-cache.json`);
  - `clip_edges(src, a0, z0)` snaps a span to silence;
  - `point(src, lo, hi, prefer)` finds the quietest cut point;
  - `_frames(src, a, b)` gives 10 ms dB frames;
  - `CLEAN_DB` is −18 dB below the body.
- Library media files are cut tight to the line, so a clip's in edge is at 0.0 s and usually not silent. Measured 2026-11-04 against `CLEAN_DB`: This Is Fine −3.4 / −6.2 dB, I am the Senate −1.3 / −5.0, Are you sure about that −15.0 / −10.4, Shut up and take my money −11.0 / −12.8; clean were only Patrick Boyle (−46.0 / −22.5), Breaking Points and Matthew Berman. G11 `meme-whole-line` fails such a clip. Fetch the longer source from `entry.source.url` (`framework/media_fetch.download`; yt-dlp is found on PATH or in the engine's tools folder by `framework/tools`) and cut it with `clip_edges` there.
- faster-whisper mishears some lines: "I am the cynic" for "I am the Senate!" (3 of 4 tokens, passes the 60% line match in `meme-whole-line`), "sometimes it may be cheap" for Gattuso's line (3 of 6, fails, so the rule reads unknown). Check `whisper_heard` against the payoff line before choosing a clip.
- Movie memes ripped from Movieclips uploads are 2.39:1 letterboxed inside 16:9 with a MOVIECLIPS.com watermark in the bottom bar (Top Gun, Training Day; three more library entries come from the same channel). Measure the picture rows (median luma > 18 over three frames; `cropdetect` is fooled by the watermark). The library now carries the frame as `picture: {x, y, w, h}` in fractions of the frame (Stonks, Skynet, Dr. Evil and I am the Senate had it by 2026-11-06); where it lacks one, record `picture: [x, y, w, h]` in source pixels and `picture_basis` on the film's MEME row (Altman `lines.json`), which overrides the library. The kit clips a fullscreen play to that rect over a matte of its own (`clip-path: inset(...)` plus a dark layer, `DD/framework/kit/compose.letterbox_inset`), so the scene underneath never shows through the bars; a cover crop to 16:9 would cut 25% of the width, faces included. The overlay modes (spotlight, slot, cutout, ghost) do not read `picture`.
- A creator clip that holds a silent beat of 1 s or more fails G11 `no-dead-air`. Measure the beat in the source (100 ms RMS) and record `cuts: [[a, b]]` in the clip's record: source ranges a jump cut drops, inside the quiet, leaving about 0.5 s of the beat. The kit emits one clip item per piece; a fullscreen piece opens with a 1.06→1 punch-in, which hides the cut (`DD/framework/kit/timeline.clip_record`).
- Overlay modes: `DD/framework/overlays.py` (size, mask, opacity, placement, motion). Spotlight discs clamp at `SPOT_Y_RANGE`; keep anchors out of the 10% band.

## Voice
- `generation_submit`, model `elevenlabs/voice`, options `{production, modelId: "eleven_v4"}` (also `stability`, `similarity`, `speed`). Leave `voiceId` out: the default voice set in Settings (Media generation) speaks; pass one only when the brand kit or Inso names another voice for the film, and keep it for every act. The tool has no endpoint, output-format or pronunciation option: it always uses the timestamps endpoint and returns `audio.mp3` plus `timing.json` (`words[{text, startSeconds, endSeconds}]`), so apply the film's pronounce map in the text you send. Submit one act per job, with at most 2 running at once. Poll `generation_status`.
- Never time-stretch the voice. The licence is `elevenlabs-unreviewed`, so say so.
- Spend guard: every `generation_submit` into the production is refused until the Video editor's `script_gate` passes and `kit_approve` records Inso's yes, both read from that production's own `receipts/` (`script-gate.json`, `kit-approval.json`).
  - Once per brand: `dd setup <brand folder>` writes `<brand>/productions/house-gates.json` from this plugin (the bun that runs it, `gate/launch.ts` in the plugin), keeping any production's `exclude`. Any change to that file makes the gate stale.
  - Once per production: `dopamine-debt.json` in its folder with `"revision": "<film>/<rev>"` (resolved under `<brand>/productions/dopamine-debt/films/`: the folder the editor's `script_gate` hashes, the house gate reads (`gate/run.ts` sets `DD_FILMS_ROOT` from the production) and `dd new-film`'s `film.py` builds from; `DD_FILMS_ROOT` overrides all three) and the `audiences`.
  - Then SKILL F-12a (`script_gate`, follow it with `job_status`) and F-12b (`generation_quote`, Inso's yes, `kit_approve` with `inputsHash` and `estimateUsd`). Any later edit to lines.json, spine.yaml or intent.json makes the approval stale; run both again before the next spend.

## Render
- One heavy render at a time. A full render takes about 18 minutes, and the hub flags it as stalled at 10 minutes, so keep waiting.
- G11 build checks: `DD/framework/g11.py` is the one implementation (`code_checks(plan)`, advisory `judge_checks(states, completion)`, `calibrate(completion)`). A kit film gets its adapter from `DD/framework/kit/g11plan.py` (`plan(film)`, `checked(film)`, `judged(film, completion)`): `kit/compose.py` writes `layout.json` (boxes, labels, picture changes, pictures, scene reveals) at compose time, and `measure_page` reads the page's text in headless Chrome or Edge (`framework/layout.py`). The Altman roast (`altman-ram-roast/build/g11.py`) is its reference caller.

## Delivery
- Mix: voice is the anchor. The bed sits at least 12 LU under the voice under the narration, ducks at least 6 dB and sits at least 18 LU under every meme that plays its own sound (the approved roast: −14.0 LU, −8.4 to −9.7 dB, −22.4 to −23.2 LU; `calibration/music/levels.json`). Memes have their own audio. Sound effects stay off narration words. Deliver at −14 LUFS and −1.5 dBTP (`delivery: youtube`). A kit film mixes with `DD/framework/kit/mix.py` at its `Film.levels` (`MixLevels`: voice −19, clips −19, bed −33 LUFS, duck −9 dB, −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP by default; the roast shipped at −2.0 dBTP).
- Music bed: a media-library music entry or a track in `DD/music.json` `approved` (G11 `music-from-library`, `music-approved-by-inso`, hard). An `approved` entry carries Inso's own yes (`inso`, his words) or, for a bed the Filmmaker made for the film, `approved_by: "standing ruling 2026-11-07"` and `film: "<production id>"` (it counts for that film only) (never a quoted yes he did not give); every entry names its licence, and an `elevenlabs-unreviewed` one goes in the delivery note. `references` are beds he praised, kept for listening; `rejected` holds his words (security-week r1's `motion_score` bed, "fucking horrible"). Never ship a `motion_score` render as the bed. Inso judges the bed by ear in the render: no connected model accepts audio input (2026-11-05), so there is no listening judge.
  - The builder fills each `Plan.music` row with `{id, file, asset, lib, bpm, mode}` and `Plan.stems` with `{voice, music, memes}`: each layer as mixed, on the film clock (`kit.mix.stems`; `calibration/music/altman.py` renders them from an ffmpeg mix graph).
  - G11 `music-no-vocals` runs silero VAD on each bed file (instrumentals 0%, a sung track 85%); never use faster-whisper text for it, it hallucinates "see you in the next video" on instrumentals.
- Final checks: decode end to end, frame count, A/V gap under 0.05 s, 1920×1080 at 30 fps.
- Keep every generation's prompt, options and job id in its asset record when the job finishes. The Altman roast's two beds (M1 about 96 bpm G major for acts 0–2; M2 C minor, switched in at line a3.1 = 6:25 by `build/roast.py` `music=[("M1", None), ("M2", "a3.1")]`) were later the part Inso singled out, and their prompts could not be recovered: the asset records held levels only.

## Cut-stage lessons (Anthropic IPO v4, 2026-11-04)
- Voice: check the ElevenLabs credits against the characters before a re-voice; a quota run-out mid-act leaves the film at its recorded length (v4: 86 credits left against 373 needed).
- Music: a prompt asking for "minor key" can come back major. Measure each bed with librosa and rank by the minor-vs-major margin, then write bpm/mode into the Plan's `music` row.
- HyperFrames: set a GSAP-tweened element's initial state with `tl.set`/`fromTo`, never an inline CSS transform (lint flags it). Build ids as uid + a letter separator, never uid + a digit (collisions).
- Cold open: the real voice can push G2 past 5 s. Tighten only measured silent runs (−40 dBFS, ≥ 0.15 s, searched between word midpoints) to 40% (`v4/make/clock.py`); never time-stretch.
- The builder's estimated boxes miss real overflow. Measure band text with `framework/layout.py` and filter text clipped by overflow windows (receipt rows) in the film's adapter (`v4/framework/g11plan.py`). Size Impact and Courier text from font metrics and clamp it out of the band in the kit.
- A persisting money object (receipt) is a text wall unless it shows only the new lines plus at most one context row.
- `meme-canonical-version` reads the library entry's `canonical` field `{is, basis, by, at}` (Inso's ruling, 2026-11-06). A placed meme fails when its entry has no `canonical` or `canonical.is` is false. Fill it from evidence with `library_tag canonical {is, basis}` (basis: the template's Know Your Meme page or its most-viewed upload); Inso's switch on the Library page writes `by: "inso"`, and evidence never overwrites it. Never hand-edit the library.
- The media-library face box can mark the wrong person in a two-shot (Primack's marked the host); look at the disc frame before trusting it.
- Re-voice only the acts whose text changed: compare `make/voice.py` `act_text(act)` with the `text` stored in `voice/jobs.json`. ElevenLabs bills about one credit per character sent (v4 A4: 2,111 characters, the same figure the quota refusal named). `generation_submit` reports cost on its unverified upper-bound basis, not the account's credits. A re-voice of one act moves only that act's rows: the clock left A0-A3 identical to the frame.
- A storyboard picture that quotes a long filing passage in `what` counts every unspoken quoted word. At 12 or more, the whole unit is charged as a wall (`wall_s`, move RE-TIME PAGE). v4 a4.1d2 had 15 such words, and that one picture edit raised the integral from 36.6 / 102.7 to 56.9 / 129.7. Put on screen the narrated words plus the proof phrase ('… X … roughly 30 global targets …'), act the number out, and keep the composition's page text the same as `what`.
- `hyperframes lint` `composition_heavy_overlay_count_high` counts every element with `radial-gradient`, `filter:blur` or `clip-path`, hidden ones included. 30 radial-gradient bullseyes tripped it, and the warning means a risk of black frames in the render. Draw repeated icons with borders and child divs instead.

## Cut-stage lessons (weight-space r1, 2026-11-06)
- **SEEN frames.** `kit.seen.frames` samples each unit at its first look and at each picture's settled state, 0.3 s before it ends (`shot_times`). The old fixed 20/50/80% frames caught a bar mid-growth (24.8% where the film lands on 48.1%), and the juror then vetoed a correct "six times" line as a wrong number (G3 0.55).
- **The blind reader is a defect finder.** Ask the fresh reader for a strict list of blank, cramped, cut-off or covered frames as well as the descriptions. Its first list found what the old G11 band and text-wall rules never measured; `no-clipped-text` and `tag-clear-of-text` now measure it on the page's held states. Run the reader again on each new render: the second pass still found 7 defects.
- **Meme jump cuts.** A meme whose own silent beat runs over 1 s takes `cuts` on its MEME row (source ranges inside that beat, leaving about 0.5 s of it). G11 treats the pieces as one play for whole-line, payoff and franchise. Measure the cut with `quiet._frames`, not Whisper: Whisper's word starts land inside the silence.
- **A voice run is broken only by a laugh between its voice segments.** `dry_voice_run` counts voice up to the last segment of a laughing bit, so a meme after the run's final line doesn't shorten it. Put the meme after the line where the run should break, and give the following lines their own bit only when the meme really punches the line before it.
- **G2 is bounded by the voiced opening line.** First laugh is the end of the first laughing bit. When the opening line alone runs past `thesis_by_s` at the real voice (weight-space: 6.3 s of speech, gaps already ≤ 0.4 s), no picture change and no silence trim can pass it: re-voice a shorter line, or take it to Inso.
- **The music bed ducks under quiet meme lines.** At the kit's 9 dB duck the bed sat 16.7 LU under Tim Robinson's quiet line, against the 18 LU of `music-drops-under-memes`; the film set `MixLevels(music_duck_db=-11)`. Re-run `mix.stems` after every timeline change, because stale stems give false music fails.
- **Check what a diagram's labels claim against the source's own frame of reference.** The Breakout grid lit row 8 under the words "bottom row"; the exp1 prompt makes rows 0 and 9 a wall border, so row 8 is the playable floor. Drawing the wall ring as a wall and labelling the row by the narration cleared the juror's 0.40 "wrong detail" read.
