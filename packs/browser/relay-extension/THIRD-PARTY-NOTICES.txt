THIRD-PARTY NOTICES - Dimension Browser Relay (Chrome extension)
==================================================================

This folder is a Chrome (Manifest V3) extension. It bundles no third-party code: background.js, options.js and
options.html are plain JavaScript and HTML with no libraries in them, and the extension uses only Chrome's own
extension APIs (debugger, tabs, tabGroups, storage, alarms).

All of it is copied from OMP and adapted for the Browser pack:

  OMP (https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi), packages/browser-relay/extension and

"dimension-relay-keepalive". The behaviour is OMP's.
