# YouTube

## What it is

A YouTube connector for your agent, plus three skills that take a finished film from a render to a published video: they package it, design its thumbnails, and upload it, through the YouTube API when the channel is connected or through YouTube Studio in your own signed-in browser when it is not.

## What it contains

- **Fifteen native tools, for any number of channels.** Every Connect adds the channel you pick at Google's sign-in (your account or any brand account it manages); `youtube_channels` lists them, `youtube_forget_channel` removes one and Disconnect removes them all. Tools on a video use the channel that owns it; playlist and comment tools take `channel` when several are connected. Nine over the YouTube Data API v3: `youtube_search`, `youtube_get_videos` (up to 50 videos per call), `youtube_list_playlists`, `youtube_list_playlist_items`, `youtube_playlist_add`, `youtube_list_comments`, `youtube_post_comment`, `youtube_get_captions`, `youtube_channel_stats` (by @handle or id). Two for your channels' own numbers: `youtube_video_analytics` (Analytics API v2: views, engaged views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, traffic sources, retention curve) and `youtube_reach_report` (Reporting API reach reports: thumbnail impressions and CTR by video or traffic source; the plugin creates the two report jobs per channel at the first session after it connects, because YouTube backfills only 30 days before a job exists). Two for your videos: `youtube_upload_video` uploads a file (resumable, public unless told otherwise, with thumbnail, playlist and captions) and reads it back, and `youtube_update_video` changes a posted video's title or thumbnail. Sign in with your own Google Cloud OAuth client (the one Drive and Calendar use works), with the YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs enabled, and publish its consent screen so the sign-in does not expire after 7 days.
- **Skill `youtube`**: how to use the tools, their quota costs and what the APIs will not do.
- **Skill `youtube-package`**: scored title options, a description with chapters built from the edit's markers, sources and credits, tags, captions, the AI-use disclosure decision, Shorts and end-screen plans. It writes `receipts/publish.json` in the production folder and checks it with `scripts/publish-check.mjs` (plain `node` or `bun`, nothing to install): chapter rules, title and description limits, file hashes, a verdict for every generated or commentary asset, credits present in the description.
- **Skill `youtube-thumbnail`**: one 1280×720 thumbnail, or three for an A/B test, as HyperFrames compositions, rendered with the Motion plugin, checked against the zones where YouTube draws over a thumbnail and at phone size with `scripts/thumbnail-pack.mjs` (needs `ffmpeg`).
- **Skill `youtube-studio-upload`**: uploads a checked package with one `youtube_upload_video` call when the channel is connected, otherwise drives Studio with the Browser plugin's `browser_run`; it records the video id and what was read back in `receipts/publish.json` (through the API: title, visibility, audience, schedule, thumbnail, playlist and captions; in Studio: every field).
- **Rule `youtube-context`**: always-on guidance for the connector tools.

## Who can use it

Off by default; turn it on in Plugins. The connector needs a Google sign-in through your own OAuth client with the YouTube Data API v3 enabled and its OAuth consent screen published (In production): a client left in Testing gets refresh tokens that expire after 7 days. The three film skills also need the Motion plugin (thumbnails), a production made in the Video space, and the Browser plugin for the Studio upload when the channel is not connected; the package and thumbnail skills run anywhere a production folder exists.

## Limits and risks

- **The API upload (`youtube_upload_video`) has run against a fake Google in tests, not yet against a real channel.** It sends the file in 8 MB parts and resumes after a dropped connection, so a long film survives a bad network. The Studio upload has run once, on 2026-10-31, against a signed-in Studio; the custom thumbnail was not seen to take. Studio changes its markup; the skill reads the page first and stops when a field will not read back.
- **It never handles your password.** Sign-in, two-step verification and CAPTCHA are yours, in the Browser View.
- **The Studio upload runs `browser_run`, which has full Node access** on your machine and uses whichever saved browser profile you let it open, including its Google sign-in. The host asks before each run. Use a profile you are happy for the agent to act in, and watch in the Browser View.
- **Public unless you said otherwise.** An upload goes up public unless the package, the campaign or you named unlisted, private or a scheduled time. It runs under the session's permission mode and asks nobody on its own; in Traction a pick on the campaign board is the go-ahead.
- **Disclosure is your decision.** The skill sets out what YouTube says must carry the AI label and records your answer with reasons; it cannot tell whether a clip looks real, and commentary clips carry a fair-use judgement that is yours.
- **Thumbnails are 1280×720.** YouTube recommends larger; the Motion plugin's stills are this size. Custom thumbnails need a verified channel. The overlay zones and the title and thumbnail statistics the skills quote are third-party measurements, labelled as such, and can change.
- **The API connector** uploads with `youtube_upload_video` (title, description, tags, category, audience, altered-content answer, language, licence, embedding, notify, schedule, thumbnail, playlist and captions; end screens, cards and Test & Compare stay in Studio). Its only edit of a posted video is `youtube_update_video`: a title or thumbnail, one change per call, under the session's permission mode; a title change resends the video's whole snippet, because videos.update wipes a description or tags it is not sent. Pinned comments have no API either. Analytics trail by about two days and reach reports arrive daily, the first up to 48 hours after the job is created, so Studio is the only source for a video's first 48 hours. A connection made before 0.4.0 lacks analytics access: reconnect. Comments are public: the tool posts at most 5 a day and refuses repeated text. Quota (as of 2026-11-06): search and uploads each have their own 100 calls a day since 2026-06-01; most other calls share 10,000 units a day at 1 unit each, a title change costs 51 (read plus update), a thumbnail 50, a playlist add 50 and a caption upload 400.
- **Captions:** with the API they go up with the video; in Studio they go through the Subtitles page and are the least certain step.

## Build and test

`bun run check` runs the formatter and linter. The two scripts are plain `.mjs` with no dependencies; their tests are in `test/` and run with `bun test`.
